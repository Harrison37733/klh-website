Engineering experience you can rely on

KLH & Associates are consulting Civil and Structural Engineers working nationally and internationally.

Our client base includes construction companies, government authorities, local authorities, water authorities, industrial organisations and commercial and private developers.

We specialise in resolving difficult engineering schemes by developing suitable designs while considering construction sequence, methodology, safety, environmental requirements, programme and cost.
