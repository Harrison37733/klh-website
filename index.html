CIVIL & STRUCTURAL ENGINEERING CONSULTANTS

KLH Consultancy Ltd

Practical civil and structural engineering consultancy for complex construction projects.

Our client base covers construction companies, government authorities, local authorities, water authorities, Network Rail, London Underground, Docklands Light Railway, industrial organisations, Harbour Authorities (civil marine works), commercial and private developers.

Explore our services

A large proportion of the technical services provided have been carried out for national construction companies, where engineering expertise in areas such as concept development, surveys, estimating, planning, construction methodology, temporary works design, safety and environmental issues is crucial to successful construction and cost-effectiveness.

In particular, we specialise in resolving difficult engineering projects by developing alternative permanent works designs or resolving construction difficulties through temporary works designs, enabling client requirements to be achieved while taking contractual implications into consideration. Services are also available for civil estimating, tenders procurement, QS duties and project management.

Our services cover the whole of the UK and Ireland, subject to agreed terms and conditions. We will make our best endeavours to deal with any requests to resolve immediate problems within the scope of our activities.

The success of our projects has been achieved through the qualified personnel who work on them, together with the considerable design, commercial and practical experience available, both pre- and post-contract.
