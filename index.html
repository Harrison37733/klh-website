KLH Consultancy

KLH Consultancy are consulting Civil and Structural Engineers working nationally and internationally.

Our client base covers Construction Companies, Government Authorities, Local Authorities, Water Authorities, Industrial Organisations, Commercial and Private Developers.

A large proportion of work is carried out for National and International Construction Companies, where technical expertise and cost effectiveness is an essential aspect of any design.

In particular we specialize in the resolving of difficult engineering schemes by development of a suitable design together with sequence of construction, methodology, safety, environmental, time and cost considerations all being taken into account.

The success of projects has only been achieved by the qualified personnel that work on them and the considerable design and practicable experience available.

Rail

KLH Consultancy involvement in rail works started in the 1980’s and covers virtually all the main railway operators in the UK.

The majority of our designs have been in connection with Network Rail, London Underground and the Docklands Light Railway.

Much of our work on many projects has required both the conception and development of Temporary and Permanent works designs with the constraints on designing components to suit live track conditions or to be carried out during track possessions.

In conjunction with the compiling and submission of the necessary documents such as Approval In Principle (AIP’s), Conceptual Design Statements (CDS) and Safe Load Assessments. Providing design to suit the necessitated detailed planning and the deriving of methods in close liaison with our clients.

KLH Consultancy have been certified as Category 2 and 3 design checkers on numerous rail projects.

The list of Rail projects relate to engineering work where KLH Consultancy have provided either fully or in part the following services:

  • Methodology of construction

  • Design construction interface management.

Geotechnical

Desk top studies of proposed sites for development is a service provided by the geotechnical division. Such a service covers full topographical, geological, contamination, environmental, flooding and drainage assessments and also probable traffic flow increase with the likely impact on the existing carriageway network.

We are able to offer full contamination and geotechnical investigations through our in house partnering arrangements with the BdR consultancy service situated within the same building as KLH Consultancy.

Interpretation of site investigation (SI) reports and consequent advice to clients to develop the most suitable design to cope with the SI findings is an exercise common to the majority of our commissions.

Contractual Consultancy

Contractual advice, together with both the submission of contractual claims and in other cases the defence of claims has been a feature of our services for many years.

Assistance in the preparation of civil engineering contractual claims have been undertaken for major International Contractors.

Comprehensive advice, contractual correspondence, compilation and the complete submission of claims have been undertaken successfully for a number of clients. These claims have generally been associated with the ICE 5th and 6th Editions of Contract, the NEC for of contract regarding compensation events, various JCT Forms of Contract, The Red and Green forms of Petro-chemical Conditions of Contract, and the ad-hoc Construction Management forms of contract, together with the associated sub-contract terms and conditions.

Claims, advice and services have encompassed civil road, rail, bridge, drainage, marine and fire protection services.

Since the 1980’s KLH Consultancy has played a major part in assisting or resolving over a dozen contract claims. In addition KLH has been nominated as an expert witness on two occasions.
