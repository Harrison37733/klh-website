PROJECTS

Land Reclamation Scheme, Dover

250,000m³ of sea-dredged ballast and 500m of two-walled cofferdam retaining structures. Constructed using overhand working techniques with attendant pontoons and tugs, with 130,000m³ of fill placed into the cofferdams. Temporary works designs and Deputy Project Manager services.

Dover Harbour – New Ro-Ro Deep-Water Berths 5 & 6

Reclamation fill of approximately 800,000 tonnes, 400m of retaining two-walled sheet-piled cofferdams, five berthing dolphin cells, each containing 2,500m³ of pumped concrete, concrete approach ramps and coastal protection reinforced-concrete units. The majority of the works were constructed using overhand techniques with attendant pontoons, tugs and barges. We acted as a resident consultant on site for design and checking duties, together with providing advice on contractual matters.

New Humber International Tanker Terminal

Compiling JV tender.

Scrabster Harbour, Scotland

Jetty calculations and drawings to substantiate a contractual claim.

South Coast Reclamation Scheme, Brighton

Tender compilation.

Isle of Wight wave wall and groynes

Project Manager for a reinforced-concrete wave wall with apron and 16 groynes on the Isle of Wight. The toe of the wave-wall apron was redesigned by us using sheet piles, saving operational cost and time.

Devonport Jetty

Full temporary works design to work off permanent piles using overhand construction techniques, with a 45-tonne crawler crane and 5-tonne piling hammer. Cast-in-situ jetty deck using jacked rolling soffit formwork.

Jack-up platforms and pontoon design

Stability checks.

Widening of Kingston Bridge

River Thames, Old BSC Jetty

Survey for demolition and method provided for the contractor.

Greenwich Millennium Project

Tender and temporary works for the south-west outfall cofferdam in the Thames.
