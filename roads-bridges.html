Roads & Bridges

Civil and Structural Engineering Consultants

Extensive experience in the construction management and technical delivery of road and bridge projects, including the complete management of a 1.2km A26 trunk road realignment and reconstruction for East Sussex County Council.

Experience includes road and bridge tenders across the UK and Ireland, Network Rail bridge works at Wembley, and the New Medway Crossing.

Other projects include bridge modifications at the Tate & Lyle factory, elevated bridge slide-track and jacking systems, Thamesmead bypass cofferdams and drainage works, three-span river bridges in Homesley and South Wales, M4 bridge demolition methodology, and the design of large water-retention caissons for Yorkshire Water.
